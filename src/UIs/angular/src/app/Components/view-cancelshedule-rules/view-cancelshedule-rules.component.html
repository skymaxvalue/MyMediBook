Appointment Cancellation Policy

Please review the cancellation policy before cancelling your appointment.

Free Cancellation

Appointments may be cancelled free of charge if the cancellation request is made more than 24 hours before the scheduled appointment time.

Late Cancellation Fee

If an appointment is cancelled within 24 hours of the scheduled appointment, a late cancellation fee may be applied.

Cancellation Not Allowed

Appointments cannot be cancelled once the scheduled appointment time has started.

Emergency Cancellation

Emergency cancellation requests may be approved at the clinic's discretion. Supporting information may be required depending on the circumstances.

Important

By booking an appointment, you acknowledge and agree to this cancellation policy. The clinic reserves the right to modify this policy when necessary.
