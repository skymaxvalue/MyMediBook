Appointment Reschedule Policy

Please review the reschedule policy before changing your appointment.

Free Rescheduling

Appointments may be rescheduled free of charge when the request is made more than 24 hours before the scheduled appointment time.

Late Reschedule Fee

If an appointment is rescheduled within 24 hours of the scheduled appointment, a late reschedule fee may apply according to the clinic's policy.

Maximum Reschedule Limit

Each appointment may be rescheduled a maximum of 2 times. Once this limit has been reached, further reschedule requests will not be permitted.

Rescheduling Not Allowed

Appointments cannot be rescheduled once the scheduled appointment time has started. A new appointment must be booked instead, subject to clinic availability.

Emergency Reschedule Requests

Emergency reschedule requests may be considered at the clinic's discretion. Supporting information may be requested depending on the circumstances.

Important

By booking an appointment, you acknowledge and agree to this reschedule policy. Selecting a new appointment date and time is subject to the doctor's availability. The clinic reserves the right to modify this policy whenever necessary.
